# Purple Gate — design brief (source of truth)

## 1. Basic idea
- Mobile idle clicker / incremental.
- Purple magic portal in a goblin camp.
- Player fires spells **into** the portal.
- Portal pays **gold**.
- Gold piles visually in the background.
- Goblins automate casting.
- Beasts (rat → minotaur → …) sent **through** the portal on timed raids (replaces ship tiers).
- Prestige slams the gate, resets camp, unlocks a magic **class** (first: necromancy).
- NOT black hole / space / shooting the portal. You feed it spells.

## 2. Title
- **Purple Gate**
- One-liner: Pixel goblins throw spells into a purple portal. Gold piles up. Send a minotaur through. Prestige into necromancy.

## 3. Platform / feel
- Mobile, portrait, one thumb.
- Pixel art, 16-bit, chunky.
- Palette: grape purple, slime green, torch orange, gold, mud brown.
- Home screen one-screenshot readable: portal, flying spells, gold hill, goblins, parked beast.
- Sessions: 15s tap+collect or 5–8 min upgrade+raid.
- Progresses while app closed.

## 4. Currency
- Primary: **gold** (1.2K, 4.5M…).
- Secondary: **Runes / Grimoire pages** (prestige); optional Stew/offline fuel as Ice/Time.
- Goblins/beasts = units, not currencies.
- No generic “loot” score.

## 5. Visual gold pile (required)
- Gold lands behind/around portal.
- Art stages: few coins → scatter → mound → hill → buried camp.
- Tap: coin arcs from swirl onto pile.
- Huge pile: sparkle crest, don’t draw thousands of coins.
- Raid return: pile grows in one gulp.
- Prestige: pile gone; new dirt; maybe one starter coin.
- Pile tint follows hottest school (ember/sparks/frost/later bone-white).

## 6. Core loop
1. Equip spell school.
2. Tap portal → fire spell in.
3. Gold out → pile.
4. Spend gold; upgrade school; unlock next.
5. Hire goblins for offline auto-cast.
6. Buy beast; timed raid through portal; gold burst.
7. When Fire+Lightning+Ice familiars run and pile huge → slam gate.
8. Keep Grimoire pages; unlock Necromancy; new city; Fire again + new class later.

## 7. Spell schools (unlock one by one)
**Fire (start):** fat gold/tap; Spark→Bigger Ember→Fireball→Meteor Spit→Campfire Familiar.
**Lightning (2nd):** faster casts; Spark Chain→Fork→Storm Rune→Bottle Imp.
**Ice (3rd):** slower chunkier gold; Shard→Hail Gate→Glacier Plug→Snow-goblin.
- Short upgrade columns, not infinite trees.
- Selected school = manual tap.
- Familiar = idle auto-cast for that school.
- Synergy flavors raids (Fire bigger gulp, Lightning faster return, Ice drip while gone).

## 8. Casting UX
- Portal center; school buttons around; tap swirl/cast button.
- No aiming / projectile skill.
- Spell goes **in**, gold comes **out**.

## 9. Goblins
Grabber, Bag-boy (optional pile helper), Hex-rat, Lookout, Stew witch, Boom-gob, Boss (2nd beast slot).
Hire with gold / raids / optional sack gacha. Idle on home screen; react to gold hill.

## 10. Raids — beasts
Timed missions. Ladder: Dire Rat → Wolf → War Boar → Troll → Minotaur → Wyvern → Hydra → Gate Titan.
Pick beast + 1–3 goblins + pocket world. Timer only — no dodge minigame.
Fail = weak gold. Beaten routes can auto-repeat.
Return: beast out of swirl, pile jumps.
Pocket worlds: mushroom cellar, night market, haunted chapel, hedge-maze, wizard attic, dragon linen cupboard.

## 11. Prestige — classes
**Slam the gate.** Lose camp/gold/schools/beasts (or reset to rat). Keep Grimoire pages, one permanent scar, unlocked classes.
Pages buy **classes**, not Fire×2.
1. Necromancy (first) — Bone school, skeleton goblin, etc.
2–5 later: Witchcraft, Demonology, Time, Name-Steal.
New city always starts with Fire.

## 12. Home layout
Portrait; portal center; gold hill behind; school ring; goblin row bottom; beast parked side; HUD gold, gold/sec, raid timer; tabs Camp/Spells/Beasts/Prestige.

## 13. First-hour unlocks
0m Spark; 5m Fireball+Grabber; 15m Fire familiar+Dire Rat; 25m Lightning; 45m Ice; later Minotaur; slam when Ice familiar online. Necromancy not in hour one.

## 14–16. Tone / monetization / do-not-build
Funny greedy goblin dump — not grimdark, not Portal puzzles, no sci-fi/black holes/lasers.
Safe monetization: ad-free, cosmetics, convenience — no raw gold multipliers that skip first prestige.
Do not: black hole combat, aiming, second primary “loot” currency, unlock all schools at once, prestige that only multiplies Fire.

## 17. Success test
Store gif works with no text: goblin fireball → purple oval → gold hill grows → minotaur into swirl → pile bigger.
